# Attempt verdict

Optional note shape under the
[attempt validation contract](../references/attempt-validation.md). Use
observable evidence, not hidden reasoning. Missing evidence stays unknown.

- Attempt/revision and relevant environment:
- Protected goal and original success criteria:
- Process: good / bad / unknown; provisional if applicable; evidence and limits:
- Outcome: good / bad / unknown; provisional if applicable; evidence and limits:
- Combined interpretation, without inferring either axis from the other:
- Material critic claims: supported / contradicted / unresolved; evidence links:
- Verifier's evidence, criteria, and premises; unchecked judgments:
- Independent methods/sources, shared origins, or unknown independence:
- Meta-verification: not needed with reason / named uncertainty and bounded
  check; result and stopping condition if performed:
- Unresolved claims and permitted next action:

MUST preserve provenance and confidence through the
[feedback evidence contract](../references/feedback-evidence.md). This verdict
MUST NOT promote a reusable rule, erase an earlier failure, authorize another
agent, or replace the existing completion/review decision.
